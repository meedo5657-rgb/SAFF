# World Cup 2026 — Foreign-Born & Naturalized Players

Source: relevant-football.com `/data/players/<country>.json` for squads & birthplaces (`birth_city`, `birth_country`).  
Naturalization status researched per-player from Wikipedia (ancestry, youth national teams, citizenship route) and reputable football media.

**Definitions:**  
- **Born abroad** = player's `birth_country` differs from the national team's country (excludes historical in-border cases: Yugoslavia/USSR/Czechoslovakia/Zaire/Netherlands Antilles).  
- **Naturalized** = acquired the nationality WITHOUT birth in-country AND WITHOUT descent (no parent/grandparent from that country) — e.g. citizenship by residence, refugees/childhood immigrants, or FIFA switches with no blood tie.  
- **Heritage** (not naturalized) = citizen by descent: at least one parent/grandparent from the country (typical diaspora player born abroad).
