📖 Rules & How it works
🎯 How points work
PointsWhenExample
6 Exact final score You predict 2–1 → match ends 2–1 (spot on)
3 Correct outcome only (right winner, or a draw) You predict 2–0 → match ends 3–1 (right that the home team wins, wrong score)
0 Wrong outcome You predict a home win → the away team wins (nothing)
🥅 Knockout matches that go to penalties
PointsWhenExample
7 Exact draw score + correct penalties winner Predict 1–1, Brazil on penalties → ends 1–1, Brazil win penalties (6 + 1)
6 Exact draw score, but wrong penalties winner Predict 1–1, wrong team on penalties → ends 1–1 (score only)
4 Wrong score, but correct draw + penalties winner Predict 2–2, Brazil → ends 1–1, Brazil win penalties (3 + 1)
3 Correct draw outcome only, but wrong penalties winner Predict 2–2, wrong team on penalties → ends 1–1 (outcome only)
0 Wrong (e.g. you predicted a win) Predict 2–1 → match ends as a draw and goes to penalties
📌 Other notes
• ✏️ You can change your prediction freely, but it stops at the match's real kickoff — no edits after the match starts.
• 🏅 Ties in the standings are settled by who has more exact-score predictions.
