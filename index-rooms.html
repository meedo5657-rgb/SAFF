📖 Rules & How it works
📊 How points work
PointsWhenExample
5 Exact score — both teams' goals correct You predict Brazil 2–1 Spain, and it ends Brazil 2–1 Spain
3 Correct result only — right winner or draw, wrong score You predict Brazil 2–0 Spain, and it ends Brazil 3–1 Spain
0 Wrong result You predict Brazil 2–1 Spain, and it ends Brazil 0–1 Spain
🥅 Knockout matches that go to penalties
PointsWhenExample
6 Exact draw score and correct penalties winner You predict Brazil 1–1 Spain, Brazil win penalties, and that is exactly what happens
5 Exact draw score, wrong penalties winner You predict Brazil 1–1 Spain, Brazil win penalties, but it ends 1–1 and Spain win penalties
4 Correct draw result and penalties winner, wrong score You predict Brazil 2–2 Spain, Brazil win penalties, and it ends Brazil 1–1 Spain with Brazil winning penalties
3 Correct draw result, wrong penalties winner You predict Brazil 2–2 Spain, Brazil win penalties, but it ends 1–1 and Spain win penalties
0 Wrong result — you predicted a winner You predict Brazil to win, but the match ends a draw and goes to penalties
🏆 Tournament-long picks
PointsWhenExample
30 Champion: you pick the team that wins the 2026 World Cup You pick Argentina, and Argentina win the final
15 Top scorer: you pick the player who finishes as the tournament's top scorer You pick Kylian Mbappé, and he finishes with the most goals
Both picks lock when the first match of the tournament kicks off — no edits after that.
📌 Other notes
• 2x cards: you get 5 for the whole tournament. Activate one on a match before kickoff to double your points there
• Predictions open 24h before kickoff; you can change them freely until the match starts — no edits after that
• Ties in the standings are settled by who has more exact-score predictions
📲 Add to your Home Screen (iPhone)
Install the app in 4 steps:
  1. Tap the 3-dot menu (•••) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" — the app icon will appear on your home screen.
📲 Install on Android
Install the app in 3 steps:
  1. Tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) at the top of Chrome.
  2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap "Install" — the icon will appear on your home screen.
